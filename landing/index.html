Grok Bot Wallpaper

A live wallpaper that keeps an eye on your cursor and on your bots.

Install on Windows Try it full screen

What it does

On a real desktop. A screen recording from Windows: Grok Bot under the desktop icons, its cards dragged into place.

It watches your cursor. The eyes follow the mouse across the whole screen. Move yours: they are on it now.

Updates itself

Always the newest version.

It loads from the web, so new versions reach your desktop on their own.

Your bots at a glance. Live cards show who works, who waits for you and who hit an error.

  • Working
  • Waiting for you
  • Error

Put the cards anywhere. Drag them across the desktop and they stay. Double-click one to send it home.

Eyes not following the cursor? In Lively, open Settings, then Wallpaper, and set Wallpaper input to Mouse. Turn on Mouse interaction when application focused so it keeps watching over open windows.

A blue box when you drag a card? That is Windows selecting desktop icons. Right-click the desktop, open View and untick Show desktop icons.

On a Mac? The Mac app is coming soon. Until then, Grok Bot runs full screen in your browser.

Your topic works like a password: keep it to yourself. Status lines go to the feed server in your link (ntfy.sh unless you set &ntfy=) and stay there for 12 hours. A self-hosted ntfy can take an access token as &auth= in that same fragment. Running the journal server instead? The steps are in README-server.md in this repository.
